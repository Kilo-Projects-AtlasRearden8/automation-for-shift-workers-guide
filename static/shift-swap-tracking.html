Stop forgetting who owes whom

Shift swaps happen on paper, in texts, and from memory — until someone forgets who owes whom. This guide shows you a tiny local tracker that keeps swaps in a plain CSV file on your computer (swaps.csv), plus what to write down for every swap so nothing slips through the cracks.

What to record for every swap

FieldWhat it means
My dateThe date of your shift being swapped
Their dateThe date of their shift being swapped
CoworkerWho you're swapping with
Statuspending, approved, denied, or done
Approved byManager or charge nurse who signed off (if required)
NotesUnit, shift type (days/evenings/nights), any conditions

Never put patient names, room numbers, or any health information in the swap tracker. It's for dates and names only.

Local tracker script (scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py)

The script keeps swaps in swaps.csv (comma-separated, easy to open in a spreadsheet) on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is hardcoded.

Add a swap:

python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py add "2026-11-07" "2026-11-10" Maya pending "Charge nurse approved"

List swaps:

python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py list

List only swaps needing attention (pending or upcoming within N days):

python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py upcoming --within 7

Mark a swap approved or done:

python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py status 1 approved
python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py status 1 done

The number (1, 2, ...) is the row number from list.

Export to a spreadsheet-friendly CSV:

python3 scripts/shift_swap_tracker.py export swaps-backup.csv

Every command defaults to a preview where it makes sense; nothing is changed until you confirm. swaps.csv lives on your computer — it's ignored by git and never meant to be committed.

Example tracker

my_date,their_date,coworker,status,approved_by,notes
2026-11-07,2026-11-10,Maya,pending,Charge nurse,Nights, unit 4B
2026-11-14,2026-11-08,Jamal,approved,J. Miller,Evenings for days

Tips

  • Add swaps as soon as they're discussed, while details are fresh.
  • Review upcoming --within 7 at the start of each week so approvals don't lapse.
  • Keep swaps.csv backed up however you back up your other files — it's just a text file.
  • Shift-swap tracking is an organizational tool, not a scheduling system. Your unit's official schedule and approval process always win.
